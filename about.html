About All Pack

A packaging house built on patience and precision.

For more than a quarter of a century we have helped ambitious brands turn a box into a statement. Our work is quiet by design, and unmistakable once you hold it.

Company heritage

From a single workbench to a full atelier.

All Pack began in 1998 as a two-person workshop in East London, hand-assembling presentation boxes for jewellers and chocolatiers who could not find the quality they wanted from large factories.

Word travelled the way it does in luxury: quietly, between people who notice details. Today we are a team of 48 designers, engineers and finishing specialists, still guided by the same belief that a package should be earned, not simply ordered.

  1. 1998

    Founded in a London workshop with one cutting table and a hot-foil press.

  2. 2008

    Opened our in-house finishing studio for foil, emboss and edge painting.

  3. 2016

    Launched our materials programme: FSC® boards and plastic-free finishes as standard.

  4. 2024

    Moved to carbon-managed production with renewable energy across our workshop.

Philosophy & values

What we believe, and what we refuse to compromise.

I

Restraint

The most luxurious design is often the one with the least on it. We edit until only what matters remains.

II

Craft

Machines give us consistency; hands give us character. We use both, and know exactly where each belongs.

III

Responsibility

Beautiful packaging should not cost the earth. We design for reuse, recycling and minimal waste from the first sketch.

IV

Candour

We tell you plainly what will work, what will not, and what it will cost. Trust is part of the finish.
