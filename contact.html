Response expectations

What happens after you write to us.

  1. Within 1 business day

    A personal reply

    A senior member of the team confirms receipt, asks any clarifying questions and suggests a time to talk.

  2. Within 3 business days

    Discovery conversation

    A 30-minute call or studio visit to understand your product, audience, finishes and schedule.

  3. Within 7 business days

    Proposal & samples

    A clear quotation with structural recommendations, plus a selection of finish samples sent to you.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

It depends on the format. Limited editions start from 100 units, rigid boxes from 300 and folding cartons from 1,000.

Can you work to an existing brand guideline?

Absolutely. We regularly translate established identities into packaging, including exact colour matching and typography.

Do you offer rush schedules?

For established designs, we can often compress production to five or six weeks. Mention your date in your brief.
