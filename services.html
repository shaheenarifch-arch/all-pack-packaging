Custom packaging design

Structure first. Surface second. Story throughout.

Our design studio works in the order that produces the best boxes: engineering the structure to fit your product perfectly, then composing the surface, then choreographing the opening.

  • Brand and customer-journey workshop
  • 3D structural concepts and animated renders
  • Dielines engineered to your exact product dimensions
  • Finish studies: foil, emboss, texture and colour matching
  • Unboxing storyboards for premium reveal sequences

Material selection & sustainability

Luxury you can feel, responsibility you can verify.

We hold sample libraries of more than 600 papers, boards and finishes, and guide you towards the choices that serve both your brand and the planet.

Fine papers

Linen, cotton, metallic and tactile embossed papers in hundreds of colours, many with recycled content.

Rigid boards

Dense, recycled greyboard in 1.5–3 mm, FSC®-certified and engineered for crisp edges.

Pulp & fibre

Moulded pulp and paper-based cushioning that replace foam and plastic, fully curbside recyclable.

Water-based finishes

Plastic-free soft-touch coatings, vegetable-based inks and recyclable foils that keep mono-material boxes recyclable.

Production & quality

Four checkpoints between your approval and your doorstep.

Our workshop is run to ISO 9001 standards, and our inspectors are trained to reject what most would pass.

  1. 1

    Incoming materials

    Every delivery is checked for colour, grain, caliper and moisture before it reaches the cutting table.

  2. 2

    In-process review

    First-off approval on every operation: print, foil, emboss, die-cut and assembly.

  3. 3

    Finishing inspection

    Foil, edges and corners are examined by hand under daylight-balanced lighting.

  4. 4

    Pre-shipment audit

    Randomised AQL 1.0 sampling, plus photographic reports shared with you before dispatch.

Timeline & process

A clear path, typically eight to twelve weeks.

You will always know what is happening and what comes next. Rush schedules are available for established designs; ask us when you get in touch.

Plan your timeline
  1. Days 1–3

    Discovery call

    We learn your product, audience, budget and launch date.

  2. Weeks 1–2

    Concept & structure

    Structural options, finish palette and a detailed quotation.

  3. Week 3

    White sample

    An unprinted prototype to test fit, feel and closure.

  4. Weeks 4–5

    Pre-production proof

    A fully finished sample for your final sign-off.

  5. Weeks 6–9

    Production

    Printing, foiling, die-cutting, assembly and four-stage inspection.

  6. Weeks 10–11

    Delivery

    Careful packing, tracked shipping and optional kitting to your warehouse.
